This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Slab Worth, visit its Terms, Privacy, or Support website, or contact the developer. Slab Worth is provided by Hayden Fuhrer. For privacy questions and requests, email haydenfuhrer2@gmail.com or visit App Support.

The important details: Your saved collection is stored on your device and may sync through your Apple iCloud account. Scanning is not entirely offline: camera card images and photos selected for identification are sent to CardSight AI. Its published terms allow uses that include AI model training. Card searches, artwork, and price lookups also contact external providers. The sections below explain these transfers, storage, and your choices.

1. Who is responsible

Hayden Fuhrer is responsible for Slab Worth's handling of personal information and, where applicable privacy law uses that term, is its data controller. Apple and other providers also process information for their own services under their applicable policies. Using those services does not remove our responsibilities for information we disclose to them.

This policy covers the App and the static Slab Worth legal and support website. It does not cover a marketplace, grading company, or other website you visit through a link, except to explain the information involved in opening that link. Our Terms of Use describe the App's license, purchases, and service limitations.

2. Information the App handles

Your collection and preferences

The App stores the cards you save, including card identifiers, names, set and collector numbers, variants, quantities, favorites, binder organization, date added, artwork links, estimated prices and sources, and price history. For graded cards, records can include the grading company, grade, and certification number you enter. It also stores portfolio history, selected settings, tutorial status, your free-scan count, and Pro access status.

These records support collection management, charts, set completion, and related features. Collection records are kept in the App's local database, with iCloud synchronization where available through your Apple account and device settings. We do not operate a separate Slab Worth server that receives a copy of your entire collection database.

Images and searches

The camera processes frames to detect a card and read text. Card images are also sent for online identification as explained in section 3. Selecting a photo for identification sends a JPEG version of that selected image. Card names, numbers, set information, language, and other search or pricing parameters are used in requests to catalog and market-data services. The receiving service can also receive your IP address and ordinary request information, such as request time and network headers.

Optional Apple profile

If you choose Sign in with Apple, the App receives an Apple-provided user identifier and, when Apple supplies it, your name. It stores these locally to show your profile. The current sign-in flow requests your name, not your email address. Your Apple password is not provided to Slab Worth, and we do not operate a separate password-based Slab Worth account service.

Purchases

Apple processes your one-time Pro purchase. The App uses Apple's StoreKit purchase and entitlement information to determine whether Pro is available, restore purchases, and recognize refunded or revoked purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide sales and transaction reports to the developer, and you may provide purchase information when asking for help.

Support and diagnostics

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any device details, screenshots, or transaction information you choose to include. Our support address uses Google's Gmail service. Apple may make diagnostic or crash information available to developers according to your Apple privacy settings and Apple's developer services. The App does not include a separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Camera and photo identification with CardSight AI

Online identification sends image content to CardSight AI. During camera scanning, the App detects and crops a card, reads text on the device, and can send repeated JPEG card images to CardSight while identification is active. Transmission can occur before you save a card. Photo identification sends a JPEG version of the photo you select; this may contain more than the card itself.

CardSight receives the submitted image and connection information needed to handle the request. The App does not attach your Apple sign-in identifier, name, or complete collection database to this identification request. However, an image can itself reveal personal information, and your IP address is visible to the receiving service. Keep people, addresses, documents, payment details, and other private material out of images you submit.

CardSight's published terms permit submitted data to be used for service improvement, research, and AI model training, and permit collection of usage information. Its public privacy policy uses purpose-based retention rather than a fixed deletion deadline. We therefore do not promise that images are immediately deleted, excluded from training, or handled only on your device. See CardSight's Terms of Service and CardSight's Privacy Policy.

CardSight's published privacy policy restricts use to people aged 18 or older. Do not use camera or photo identification if you are under 18. These restrictions apply to the online identification features, and an App Store content rating does not override them.

To avoid sending images to CardSight, do not use camera or photo identification. You can use text search to find cards; text searches still make the catalog and pricing requests described in this policy. Stop scanning and close the scanner to stop new camera-image requests. Disabling camera permission prevents camera access but does not prevent a photo you separately select from being sent for identification.

This policy provides notice of these practices. Reading it, accepting the Terms, or granting camera access does not replace any separate consent required for sharing information with an AI provider. For a request concerning an image already sent, contact us as described in sections 7 and 13.

We use information to identify cards, retrieve artwork and market estimates, save and organize your collection, calculate portfolio history, synchronize through iCloud, display your profile, verify Pro access, deliver requested notifications and widgets, provide support, diagnose errors, protect the App, and meet legal obligations. CardSight's additional uses are disclosed in section 3.

Where a legal basis is required, processing necessary to provide a feature you request is based on performing our agreement with you. Optional processing that requires consent must rely on your consent. Support, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service security may rely on our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights. Records required by law are handled to comply with legal obligations. These bases do not eliminate a separate consent requirement that applies to a particular transfer or feature.

You may decline to provide optional information, although a feature may be unavailable without the information it needs. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent as described below. Card recognition and valuation use automated processing, but the App does not make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Who receives information

Information is disclosed according to the feature you use:

  • Apple: purchases and restoration, optional Sign in with Apple, iCloud storage and synchronization, and platform services. Apple's handling is explained in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • CardSight AI: images and technical request information for online identification, with the additional uses described in section 3.
  • Pokémon TCG API, TCGdex, and PriceCharting: card identifiers, search terms, language or set details, and technical requests needed for catalog information, artwork, and prices.
  • eBay: card-related search or listing requests when its services are used. Opening an eBay listing or search also subjects that visit to eBay's practices.
  • Artwork hosts and RareBit: requests for card artwork reveal the requested image or card identifier and connection information. RareBit is an additional artwork source when that integration is configured and used.
  • Google's Gmail service: messages and attachments sent to the support email address.
  • GitHub: visits to the legal and support website, as explained in section 9.
  • Recipients you select: information you export or share through your device's share controls goes to the app, service, or person you choose.

We may disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with valid legal process, protect legal rights, investigate fraud or security issues, or protect people's safety. If responsibility for the App changes through a sale or similar transaction, relevant records may transfer subject to applicable law and notice of a material change in their handling.

Our requirements for providers handling personal information on our behalf are purpose-limited use, appropriate security, and protection consistent with this policy and applicable law. Providers that determine their own processing purposes, including CardSight's published additional uses, are identified here so those uses are not hidden behind a general reference to service providers. We remain responsible for meeting the obligations that apply to our selection and use of providers.

6. Storage and retention

We use the following retention rules rather than a single period for every type of information:

  • Collection and history: saved on your device until deleted or the App's data is removed. Synced records and device backups may remain with Apple until removed through the applicable Apple controls. Removing a card does not automatically erase historical portfolio totals.
  • Scan images: the App processes camera and selected-photo images for recognition and does not save the submitted original as part of your collection record. CardSight's retention is governed by its practices described in section 3. A source photo already in your photo library remains there until you delete it yourself.
  • Cached artwork and results: the App and operating system may cache downloaded artwork and responses for performance. Some caches last for the session; others remain until evicted by the system or the App's local data is removed.
  • Profile and settings: kept locally until changed, cleared by the relevant control, or removed with the App's local data. Signing out clears the locally stored Apple profile name and identifier.
  • Purchase records: Apple retains purchase records under its policies. The App retains or refreshes entitlement information as needed to provide Pro access. Deleting collection data does not delete an Apple purchase.
  • Support records: retained only as long as needed to address the request, provide relevant follow-up, handle a dispute, or meet a legal obligation. We assess retention using the nature of the request, whether it remains unresolved, applicable recordkeeping duties, and relevant claim periods.
  • Provider and hosting records: retained by the relevant provider under its applicable policy and legal obligations; we do not promise a common deletion deadline for records outside our control.

Where we must retain information after a deletion request, we will explain the reason and applicable retention criteria unless law prevents us from doing so. We will not retain it for unrelated purposes.

7. Access, export, deletion, and sign-out

Export your collection: Use Export CSV from the collection menu. Your chosen destination controls the exported copy. Deleting records in Slab Worth does not delete an export you previously saved or shared.

Delete cards: In your collection, open a card's context menu and choose Delete, or use Select Cards to select and delete multiple records. Deletion can propagate to synchronized devices. Associated card price history is removed when no remaining record uses that card and variant; portfolio history can remain. Deleting a binder does not by itself delete its cards.

Remove your local Apple profile: Use Sign Out in the App's profile area. This clears the profile name and identifier stored by the App. It does not erase your collection, remove iCloud data, refund Pro, or revoke the Apple sign-in association. To revoke that association, use Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple, select Slab Worth or its developer, and follow Apple's removal steps. See Apple's sign-in management instructions.

Remove remaining device and cloud data: Deleting the App removes its ordinary local app data; offloading it can preserve that data. Use Apple's iCloud storage and backup controls to review and remove cloud copies separately. Disabling synchronization is different from deleting an existing cloud copy, and signing out of the App is different from disabling iCloud. Previously created exports, shared files, source photos, and other devices' copies need separate attention. Contact support if you need help locating remaining data; we do not have administrative access to your private iCloud collection.

Request deletion of information held by us or a provider: Email haydenfuhrer2@gmail.com with the subject Slab Worth privacy request. Identify the relevant support conversation or, for a scan, the approximate date and time and feature used. Do not resend a sensitive image solely to identify your request. We will handle information we control and assist with relevant provider requests. We cannot promise recovery or identification of every provider-side request when it was not linked to an identifiable Slab Worth account.

Deleting data or stopping use does not request a purchase refund. Refund information is in the Terms of Use.

8. Permissions, notifications, widgets, and sharing

Camera access is used to scan cards. Photo selection gives the App the photo you choose for identification. You can change applicable permissions in your device's settings. Denying camera access does not prevent text search or viewing records already saved.

If you allow notifications, the App can display local alerts about card or portfolio price changes. Your widget can show portfolio value, card count, and recent history using data shared locally between the App and its widget. Information displayed in notifications or widgets may be visible to someone using or looking at your device. Control notification previews in your device's settings and remove the widget if you do not want this information displayed.

The App uses device sharing controls for exports and shared card images. Review the content and chosen recipient before sharing. Withdrawing permission stops the corresponding future access; it does not automatically erase information already sent. A consent withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

9. Advertising, tracking, and this website

Slab Worth does not include advertising networks, cross-app advertising tracking, or a separate third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your collection records for money or use your collection to serve targeted advertisements. Third-party services still receive the requests described above; the absence of advertising in the App does not mean no information leaves the device.

The Terms, Privacy, and Support website consists of static pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, forms, cookies, or browser-storage code. We do not use this website to track visitors across other websites. Its behavior does not change in response to a browser's Do Not Track signal. If an applicable privacy law requires us to honor another recognized opt-out signal, we will do so for processing within its scope.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Clicking an email link opens your email service; clicking an external link connects you to that provider.

12. Children and teenagers

Children and teenagers should not submit images or personal information to that service through Slab Worth. A parent or guardian's agreement to the Terms does not override a provider's age restriction.

For other features, any parental authorization required by applicable law must be obtained. Parents and guardians can contact us to ask about information concerning a child, request its deletion, or report an inappropriate transfer. If we learn that a child's personal information was processed without legally required authorization, we will take steps to stop that processing and delete information we control, and seek appropriate action from recipients where necessary. We do not knowingly sell children's personal information or use it for targeted advertising.
