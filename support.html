Get help with Slab Worth, report a problem, or suggest an improvement. Contact the developer using the email above, or find guidance below.

1. Send a support request

Email haydenfuhrer2@gmail.com with a short description of the issue. To help us understand what happened, include:

  • Your device model, operating-system version, and Slab Worth version, if available.
  • The steps that led to the issue and any error message you saw.
  • A screenshot, if useful, with private information hidden.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary personal information. You can also email general feedback or feature suggestions.

2. Subscriptions, purchases, and refunds

Manage or cancel Pro: Open Settings on your Apple device, tap your name, and select Subscriptions. You can also use Apple's subscription management page. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Restore Pro access: Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account used for your purchase, then use Restore Purchases in Slab Worth. If your subscription still is not recognized, contact us and describe the problem.

Request a refund: For purchases billed through the App Store, submit your request through Apple's Report a Problem service. Apple handles refund eligibility and processing under its applicable terms and local law. Contact us if paid features are not working.

3. Scanning, card identification, and prices

If a scan is unclear, try a well-lit photo with the entire card visible and reduce glare. Confirm that Slab Worth has camera permission in your device settings when using the camera.

Check the card's set, number, language, printing, variant, condition, and grading label against the result. If a match or price looks incorrect, email the card details and explain the mismatch so we can investigate.

Market prices and grading suggestions are estimates. They do not certify authenticity, guarantee a grade, or promise a sale price. Read the Terms of Use for details.

4. Collections, export, and iCloud

For help with missing collection records, CSV exports, or optional iCloud synchronization, contact us. If syncing is involved, tell us which devices are affected and whether they use the same Apple Account; you do not need to send the account's email address or credentials.

If your records are accessible, save a CSV export before deleting or reinstalling the App or making changes that could remove collection data. An export and iCloud synchronization may serve different purposes; neither should be assumed to recover all deleted information.

5. Crashes and other technical issues

Try closing and reopening Slab Worth, checking your internet connection for online features, and installing an available App Store update. If the problem continues, email the steps to reproduce it along with your device and app version. Avoid deleting the App as a first troubleshooting step if you have collection records you need to keep.

6. Privacy questions

For privacy questions or help with your information, email haydenfuhrer2@gmail.com. Describe your request without including unnecessary personal information. See the Privacy Policy draft for the current document and its review status.

Subscription cancellation is separate from deleting the App or collection information. Use Apple's subscription controls to manage future billing.
